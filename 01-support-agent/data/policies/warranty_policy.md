# Warranty Policy

Every product sold by Pixel & Plug (except gift cards) comes with a warranty against manufacturing defects. This page explains what is covered and how to make a claim.

## Warranty periods

The warranty starts on the date of delivery.

| Category | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Phones | **12 months** |
| Laptops | **12 months** |
| Wearables (smartwatches, fitness bands) | **12 months** |
| Audio (earbuds, headphones, speakers, microphones) | **6 months** |
| Accessories (cases, chargers, cables, power banks) | **6 months** |
| Gift cards | **No warranty** |

You can see the warranty period of each product on its product page.

## What is covered

The warranty covers defects in materials and manufacturing that appear during normal use. Examples:

- A screen that flickers or shows dead pixels.
- A battery that does not charge or loses charge very quickly.
- Buttons, ports or speakers that stop working.

## What is not covered

The warranty does **not** cover:

- **Physical damage**, such as cracked screens, dents or broken parts caused by drops or impact.
- **Water or liquid damage**, even for devices marketed as water resistant.
- Damage from using unofficial chargers or accessories.
- Normal wear and tear, such as scratches on the case.
- Repairs or changes made by anyone other than an authorised service centre.
- Loss or theft.

## Warranty claim or refund?

If an item is defective on arrival and you report it within **10 days of delivery**, you get a full refund under our Returns Policy.

After 10 days, a defect is handled as a **warranty claim**. A warranty claim gives you a **repair or replacement**, not a refund.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. **Contact support** with your order ID and a short description of the problem. Photos or a short video help.
2. Support checks that the product is within its warranty period and that the problem is covered.
3. If the claim is accepted, we arrange a free pickup from your address, or we give you the address of the nearest authorised service centre.
4. The service centre inspects the product. Inspection usually takes 3–5 working days.
5. We **repair** the product. If a repair is not possible, we **replace** it with the same or an equivalent model.
6. The repaired or replaced product is shipped back to you free of charge.

A replacement product is covered for the rest of the original warranty period.

## Before you send a device

- Back up your data. Repairs can erase all data on the device.
- Remove SIM cards, memory cards and cases.
- Sign out of all accounts and disable device locks.

## Questions

Contact support during support hours (9 AM–9 PM UTC) for help with a warranty claim.
