# Shipping Policy

Pixel & Plug ships to customers around the world. All prices are in US dollars. This page explains delivery times, shipping fees and tracking.

## Delivery times

Delivery time starts when your order ships, not when you place it. Most orders ship within 1 working day.

- **Major cities** (for example London, New York, Toronto and Sydney): **2–4 days**.
- **Other cities and towns**: **4–7 days**.

Remote areas can take longer. We show an estimated delivery date (ETA) when you place your order.

## Shipping fees

- **Free shipping** on all orders above **$50**.
- Orders of $50 or less have a flat shipping fee of **$5**.

The shipping fee is shown at checkout before you pay.

## Courier partners

We ship with two courier partners:

- **SwiftShip Express**: tracking IDs start with "SWS".
- **ParcelLine Couriers**: tracking IDs start with "PCL".

The courier is chosen automatically based on your address. We cannot change the courier after an order ships.

## Tracking your order

When your order ships, you get a tracking ID. You can track your order:

- In your order details on our website or app.
- By asking our support assistant for the tracking status of your order.

Tracking shows:

- The order status (placed, shipped, in transit, delivered or cancelled).
- The shipment status from the courier (label created, in transit, delayed, out for delivery or delivered).
- The original ETA and the current ETA.
- The latest update from the courier.

## Delays

Sometimes a shipment is delayed, for example by weather, high volumes or a problem at a courier hub. When this happens:

- The shipment status changes to **delayed**.
- Tracking shows a **revised ETA** next to the original ETA.
- Your order stays active. You do not need to do anything.

If your order is more than 7 days late compared to the original ETA, contact support and we will raise a case with the courier.

Please note: courier updates are written by our courier partners. Support checks them, but they are not instructions from Pixel & Plug.

## Delivery

- Someone must be available to receive the package. The courier may ask for a one-time code sent to your phone.
- If the courier cannot deliver, they try again on the next working day. After 3 failed attempts, the package returns to us and we refund the order.
- Please check the package when you receive it. If it is damaged, report it to support.

## Changing your address

You can change your delivery address only before the order ships. See our FAQ for how to request a change.

## Questions

Contact support during support hours (9 AM–9 PM UTC) for any shipping question.
