# Returns Policy

Last updated: this policy applies to all orders placed on Pixel & Plug.

We want you to be happy with every purchase. If something is not right, this page explains when and how you can return it.

## Return window

You can return most items within **10 days of delivery**. The 10 days start on the date the courier marks your order as delivered. You can see this date in your order details.

After 10 days, we cannot accept a return for a change of mind. If the item is faulty after this period, please see the warranty section below.

## Condition of returned items

To be accepted, a returned item must be:

- **Unused**, with no signs of wear, scratches or damage.
- **In its original box**, with all accessories, manuals, cables and free gifts that came with it.
- Free of any personal data. Please sign out of all accounts and reset the device before you send it back.

We check every return when it reaches our warehouse. If the item is used, damaged or missing its original box, we may refuse the return and send the item back to you.

## Items that cannot be returned

Some items are not eligible for return:

- **Gift cards are non-returnable** and cannot be refunded or exchanged for cash.
- Items that you damaged after delivery.
- Items with a broken seal on software or downloadable content.

## Defective on arrival

Sometimes an item does not work when it arrives. We call this "defective on arrival".

- Report the defect within **10 days of delivery** to get a **full refund**.
- For a defective item, the **original box is not required**. We still recommend that you keep all accessories.
- Please describe the defect clearly, for example "the screen flickers" or "the left earbud does not charge". This helps us process your request quickly.

**After 10 days**, a defect is handled as a **warranty claim**, not a refund. Under a warranty claim, the item is repaired or replaced. See our Warranty Policy for coverage and the claim process.

## How to start a return

Returns and defect reports are made **through support**. You can chat with our support assistant or contact our support team during support hours.

1. Tell support your order ID and which item you want to return.
2. For a normal return, confirm that the item is unused and in its original box.
3. For a defective item, describe the problem.
4. Support checks your order and confirms whether the return is accepted.
5. Our courier partner collects the item from your delivery address, usually within 2 working days.

## Refunds for returns

When your return is accepted, we refund you to your original payment method. Refund timelines, approval rules and limits are described in our Refund Policy. Only one refund can be made for each order.

## Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. To get a different model or colour, return the original item (if eligible) and place a new order.

## Questions

If you are not sure whether your item can be returned, contact support with your order ID. We are happy to help.
