# Refund Policy

This page explains how refunds work at Pixel & Plug: how they are approved, how much you can get back and how long the money takes to reach you.

## When you can get a refund

You can get a refund when:

- A return is accepted under our Returns Policy (item unused, in its original box, within 10 days of delivery).
- An item is defective on arrival and you report it within 10 days of delivery.
- An order is cancelled before it ships.

Gift cards are non-returnable and are never refunded.

## Approval

- Refunds of **$100 or less** are processed automatically once your request is accepted.
- **Refunds above $100 require manager approval.** A store manager reviews the request, usually within one working day. The manager may approve the refund, approve a different amount, or reject the request with a reason.

We tell you the result as soon as the decision is made.

## Refund amount

- A refund **can never exceed the order total**.
- For a full return of a single-item order, the refund is normally the full amount you paid.
- **Only one refund per order.** If an order has already been refunded, we cannot refund it again. If a refund request is rejected, you can still make a new, valid request for the same order.

## Refund timelines

Refund timelines count **from the moment the refund is processed**, not from the moment you ask for it. If your refund needs manager approval, it is processed after the approval. The time for the approval is not part of the timelines below.

After processing, the money reaches you in:

| Payment method | Time to reach you |
|---|---|
| Credit or debit card | **5–7 business days** |
| PayPal | **2–3 business days** |
| Bank transfer | **3–5 business days** |
| Pay later | **7 business days**, and your remaining payments are adjusted |

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

### Pay later refunds

For pay later orders, we cancel or reduce the payments you still owe. Money you already paid goes back to the card you paid it with.

### Card refunds

Card refunds go back to the same card. Your bank may take a little extra time to show the refund on your statement.

## Store credit

Some customers prefer store credit instead of money back. If you prefer store credit, tell support. We note your preference for future requests.

## Refund status

When your refund is processed, support confirms the amount and the expected timeline. If the money has not arrived after the timeline above, contact support with your order ID and we will check with our payment partner.

## Questions

Contact support during support hours (9 AM–9 PM UTC) for any refund question.
