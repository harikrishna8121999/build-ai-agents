# Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often at Pixel & Plug.

## Orders

**Can I cancel my order?**
Yes, but **only before the order ships**. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You can return it after delivery if it is eligible under our Returns Policy. To cancel an order that has not shipped yet, contact support with your order ID. If you already paid, the refund follows our Refund Policy timelines.

**Can I change my delivery address?**
You can change the delivery address **only before the order ships**. To request a change, **contact our support team during support hours** with your order ID and the new address. The support assistant in chat **cannot change addresses**. After an order ships, the address cannot be changed.

**How do I check my order status?**
Ask our support assistant, or open your order details on our website or app. You can see the order status, the shipment status and the estimated delivery date.

**Can I add items to an order I already placed?**
No. Please place a new order for the extra items.

## Payments

**Which payment methods do you accept?**
We accept credit and debit cards, PayPal, bank transfer and pay later.

**Can I pay later?**
Yes. Choose **pay later** at checkout to split the price into 4 monthly payments. **Pay later is available for orders up to $1,000.** For orders above $1,000, please pay by card, PayPal or bank transfer.

**Is it safe to pay online?**
Yes. All payments are handled by a certified payment partner. We never store your full card number.

## Shipping

**Is shipping free?**
Shipping is free on all orders above $50. Orders of $50 or less have a flat $5 shipping fee.

**How long does delivery take?**
Major cities take 2–4 days and other cities take 4–7 days after the order ships.

## Returns, refunds and warranty

**How long do I have to return an item?**
10 days from delivery. The item must be unused and in its original box. Gift cards are non-returnable.

**How long does a refund take?**
It depends on your payment method. Timelines start when the refund is processed. See our Refund Policy for details.

**My product stopped working after a few months. What can I do?**
Make a warranty claim through support. See our Warranty Policy.

## Account and support

**Do I need an account to order?**
Yes. An account lets you track orders and contact support about them.

**What are your support hours?**
Our support team is available **9 AM–9 PM UTC**, every day. The chat assistant can answer questions about your orders and our policies at any time.

**How do I contact a human?**
Ask the chat assistant to connect you to the support team during support hours, or email support with your order ID.
