What the LLM actually receives
# sent again, in full, every time you press Enter
messages = [
SystemMessage("You are the support assistant for Pixel & Plug..."),
HumanMessage("Where is my order ORD-1003?"),
AIMessage("Your smartwatch is on its way, but..."),
HumanMessage("Thanks! Can I still cancel it?"),
]
reply = llm.invoke(messages)
The LLM itself remembers nothing. The app keeps the list and sends the whole conversation every time. That's how it "remembers" what you said earlier, and why very long chats get slow.
System = the job description, written by developers  ·  Human = what you type  ·  AI = what the model replies
