What happens inside one LLM call

LangChain turns our messages into one long text. The LLM reads it and writes what comes next.
1. Our messages
SystemMessage
You are the customer support assistant for Pixel & Plug...
HumanMessage
Where is my order ORD-1003?
chat template special tokens mark who said what
2. The text the LLM really sees
<|im_start|>system You are the support assistant...<|im_end|> <|im_start|>user Where is my order ORD-1003?<|im_end|> <|im_start|>assistant <think></think> ▶ The LLM continues the text from here, piece by piece.
LLM writes the most likely next words
3. The reply
Your order ORD-1003 is currently being processed and should be shipped within the next 24 hours.<|im_end|>
It stops when it writes <|im_end|>. LangChain removes the tokens and gives us:
AIMessage
Your order ORD-1003 is currently being processed...
Notice: the LLM never looked anything up. It only predicted text that sounds like a support reply. "Shipped within 24 hours" is made up. This is called hallucination, and it's why the agent needs tools.
